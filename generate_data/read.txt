
KARNATAKA SYNTHETIC FIR DATASET DOCUMENTATION

1. DATASET VOLUME & SAMPLING BASELINE
-------------------------------------
* Baseline Representation:
  - Total represented police stations: 91
  - Projected baseline volume: ~20,000 FIRs/year * 16 years = ~320,000 FIRs since 2010
* Generated Dataset Size:
  - 330,000 synthetic FIR records (representing an approximate 10% sample of the baseline)
  - Output File: karnataka_fir_330k.jsonl (one JSON object per line)
  - Synthesized via a hybrid model consisting of:
    - Phase 1 (LLM Seeds): ~1,700 culturally authentic narrative seeds generated using an LLM (Ollama llama3.1:8b).
    - Phase 2 (Python Augmentation): Deterministic expansion to reach the exact 330,000 target by scaling across combinations of districts, timeline parameters, and identities while strictly keeping data logical and relational.

3. GEOGRAPHIC COVERAGE & SPATIAL MODELING
-----------------------------------------
* Districts Covered:
  All 31 districts of Karnataka:
  Bagalkot, Ballari, Belagavi, Bengaluru Rural, Bengaluru Urban, Bidar, Chamarajanagar, 
  Chikkaballapur, Chikkamagaluru, Chitradurga, Dakshina Kannada, Davanagere, Dharwad, Gadag, 
  Hassan, Haveri, Kalaburagi, Kodagu, Kolar, Koppal, Mandya, Mysuru, Raichur, Ramanagara, 
  Shivamogga, Tumakuru, Udupi, Uttara Kannada, Vijayapura, Vijayanagara, Yadgir.
* Police Stations:
  - 91 simulated police stations distributed across the state.
  - Assigned round-robin to ensure balanced geographic coverage.
* Geospatial Coordinates:
  - Police station locations are bound to district centroids.
  - Crime scenes are programmatically placed using spatial jittering (0.01 to 0.07 degrees offset from the district center).
  - Distance (km) and compass direction (e.g., North-East) from the station to the crime scene are calculated dynamically using the Haversine formula and bearing calculations.
  - Crime scene text contains authentic locality/ward names (15 per district), street names (urban/rural specific), and landmarks.

4. TEMPORAL BOUNDARIES & LEGAL REGIME PIVOT
-------------------------------------------
* Temporal Window: January 1, 2010, to June 4, 2026.
* Chronological Integrity:
  - The date and time the FIR was filed ("filed_at") represents the master timeline node.
  - The incident's occurrence window is programmatically generated to ensure the end of the occurrence ("to_time") is always prior to or equal to "filed_at".
  - If the delay in reporting ("delay_in_reporting_hours") exceeds 24 hours, a culturally-grounded narrative reason is assigned (e.g., Cauvery water agitation bandh, harvest season, local panchayat mediation, etc.).
* Legal Pivot (July 1, 2024):
  - FIRs filed BEFORE July 1, 2024, are charged under the Indian Penal Code (IPC).
  - FIRs filed ON or AFTER July 1, 2024, are charged under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS).
  - Special legislation (e.g., IT Act 2000 for Cyber Fraud) is exempt from the pivot.

5. LOGICAL GUARDRAILS & RELATIONAL INTEGRITY
--------------------------------------------
* Gender and Guardian Prefix Alignment:
  - If Gender is Male, the guardian prefix must be "S/O" (Son of) or "S/O Late".
  - If Gender is Female, the guardian prefix must be "D/O" (Daughter of) or "W/O" (Wife of).
  - If a female's prefix is "W/O", her age must be >= 18.
* Age Discrepancies:
  - Parents/guardians must be at least 18 years older than their children (e.g., if relation_to_victim is "parent", the complainant's age must be >= victim's age + 18).
* Suo Moto Consistency:
  - If the information type is "Suo Moto", the complainant is always a police officer (PSI, PI, SI, etc.), and the relation_to_victim is set appropriately.
* Stolen/Damaged Property:
  - Property details and valuation are generated only for crimes where property is stolen, damaged, or defrauded (Theft, Burglary, Property Damage, Cyber Fraud, Cheating).
* Case Investigation Status:
  - Status is assigned based on the age of the FIR. Recent cases (under 30 days old) are mostly "open" or "under_investigation". Older cases are likely "charge_sheet_filed" or "closed".
  - A court case number is only generated if the status is "charge_sheet_filed" or "closed".

6. DATA SCHEMA & JSON STRUCTURE
------------------------------
Each record in the dataset is a single JSON line adhering to the following nested structure:

* `fir_id` (int): Unique incremental ID (1 to 25000).
* `fir_number` (string): Standard police format "XXXX/YYYY" (e.g., "0025/2018").
* `gd_reference` (string): General Diary entry reference.
* `station` (object):
  - `name` (string): Name of the police station (e.g., "Police Station No. 12").
  - `district` (string): One of the 31 Karnataka districts.
  - `lat` (float), `lng` (float): Geographic coordinates of the station.
* `timeline` (object):
  - `filed_at` (string): ISO timestamp when the FIR was logged.
  - `occurrence_window` (object): ISO start and end timestamps (`from_time`, `to_time`).
  - `delay_in_reporting_hours` (int): Elapsed hours.
  - `reason_for_delay` (string/null): Factual justification if delay > 24 hours.
* `information_type` (string): "Oral", "Written", or "Suo Moto".
* `crime_type` (string): High-level category (e.g., "Cyber Fraud").
* `charges` (array of objects):
  - `act` (string): e.g., "IPC", "BNS", "IT Act 2000".
  - `section` (string): Section of the law (e.g., "303(2)").
* `description` (string): Structurally rich narrative combining incident details, victim details, evidence, and local context.
* `location` (object):
  - `text` (string): Full crime scene address including district and landmarks.
  - `distance_from_ps_km` (float): Distance to the police station.
  - `direction_from_ps` (string): Cardinal direction from the station.
  - `lat` (float), `lng` (float): Coordinates of the crime scene.
* `status` (string): "open", "under_investigation", "charge_sheet_filed", or "closed".
* `people` (object):
  - `complainant` (object): `name`, `guardian_name`, `age` (>=18), `gender`, `phone`, `relation_to_victim`.
  - `victims` (array of objects): List containing `name`, `guardian_name`, `age`, `gender`, `address`, `injury_type`.
  - `accused` (array of objects): List containing `name`, `is_known`, `physical_desc`.
* `property_stolen` (object):
  - `items_description` (string/null): Details of the property.
  - `estimated_value_inr` (int): Valued in INR.
* `investigation` (object):
  - `io_name` (string): Investigating officer name & rank.
  - `io_id` (string): Unique identifier for the officer.
  - `evidence_logged` (array of strings): Physical, digital, and witness evidence list.
  - `court_case_no` (string/null): Court record case number.
